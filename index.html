Why the flower you picked suits you.

They count in Fibonacci.

The seeds in the center aren't scattered — they're packed in two interlocking spirals, and the counts almost always come out to 34, 55, 89. A sunflower is quietly doing maths on your windowsill.

They follow the sun.

Young sunflowers tilt their faces east at sunrise and west by sunset, every day, until the blooms get too heavy. The word for it is heliotropism. Grown ones settle facing east, so they warm up fast in the morning.

Four and a half thousand years of practice.

Indigenous peoples in what is now the central United States and northern Mexico domesticated Helianthus annuus around 2,500 BC — for food, oil, dye, and medicine.

In flower language — loyalty.

Victorian florists sent sunflowers to mean loyalty, adoration, longevity — a compliment meant to last. I'm sending them for the same reason.
